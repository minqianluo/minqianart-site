About the artist

Hello, I’m Minqian

Minqian is a Los Angeles–based artist and illustrator with twenty years of painting behind her and a BFA in Illustration from Otis College of Art and Design. Her work captures the people, pets, places, and moments you love in custom hand-painted acrylic, with bright, warm brushwork that turns each one into a one-of-a-kind keepsake to treasure for a lifetime.

How a custom portrait comes to life

I

Share your photo

Choose a size and frame, then upload a clear photo where the eyes are visible.

II

Approve the sketch

Within 3 days you receive a composition sketch. Two rounds of changes are free.

III

Painted & framed

I hand-paint it in acrylic, film the process for you, frame it and ship it free.
